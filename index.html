🚨 #1 Vietnam Cash Trap to Watch For

The "Blue Polymer Note" Lookalike Trap: 500,000 VND vs. 20,000 VND

Vietnam's highest bill (500,000 VND ≈ ₹1,667) and one of its smallest bills (20,000 VND ≈ ₹67) are both greenish-blue polymer with Ho Chi Minh's portrait. In dim night markets or taxis, tourists accidentally hand over a 500k note thinking it's 20k—or dishonest street vendors/cabbies swap your 500k note via sleight-of-hand and claim you handed them a 20k note!

✅ Family Fix: Keep 500,000 VND notes in a separate zippered pocket. Whenever handing over a 200k or 500k note, say the amount out loud ("Five hundred thousand") and hold it under light before releasing it.
